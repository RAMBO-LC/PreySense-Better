**[hammadzaigham](https://github.com/hammadzaigham)**, itself adapted from
**[G-Helper](https://github.com/seerge/g-helper)** by seerge.

Full credit for the original Acer WMI/AcerService reverse-engineering and base control logic

keyboard lighting with live preview, live system monitoring, and dedicated hardware-key support
for the Acer Predator Helios Neo 16.
